Privacy policy

In short: your files stay on your Mac.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The MacClean app

MacClean scans, analyzes and cleans entirely on your Mac. File names, paths, file contents, photos, browsing history and scan results never leave your computer. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Chat Cleanup reads only the names, sizes and dates of files in the WeChat, QQ and WeCom data folders, and makes thumbnails on your Mac. It never reads chat databases or message contents, and nothing is uploaded.

The app goes online only in these cases:

  • Activating, validating and deactivating a license: it sends the license key, your Mac’s model and computer name (so you can tell your devices apart in the activation list), a randomly generated instance ID and the MacClean version.
  • Checking for MacClean updates: it asks thulem.com for update information, including the current version number.
  • Checking other apps for updates (only when you use this in App Manager): it contacts the update URLs those apps’ developers publish, the same request those apps make themselves.
  • Security checking whether certificates are revoked: macOS contacts Apple’s certificate status service, the same check it makes when you open an app. Everything else is read locally from system settings, permission records and signatures.
  • Optional usage statistics (off by default): only after you enable them, MacClean sends thulem.com a random installation ID, MacClean and macOS versions, and event names and timestamps for first successful open, daily activity and the first completed scan. Daily activity means launching or using the app in the foreground. A completed scan with no results counts; cancelled or failed scans do not. No file names, paths, contents, scan results, computer names, hardware serial numbers or license keys are sent. Statistics are stored separately from orders and licenses. The event table does not store IP addresses, and installation IDs are keyed-hashed on the server for deduplication.

Enable or disable this in the welcome guide or Settings → General → Privacy → Share usage statistics. Turning it off cancels in-flight requests, stops future reporting and clears unsent events; it cannot retract events already received by the server. The random ID and deduplication markers for sent events remain on your Mac to avoid recounting. Offline events stay locally for up to 30 days and are retried while statistics are enabled. Activity while disabled is not reported later.

These statistics help us understand successful launches, daily use and first completed scans. They cover only supported versions whose users opted in. The first report may come from an upgrade and is not evidence of a new installation. Older versions cannot be measured retrospectively, and a download request does not prove a completed download or installation.

The website and purchases

  • Orders: we store your email, plan, payment method, payment code and order time to match payments, issue licenses and help you find your order. We don’t send marketing email, and we never sell or share it.
  • Payment: you pay inside WeChat Pay or Alipay. We only see the amount received, the note you wrote and the payer nickname the platform shows us, never your payment account details.
  • Access logs: the server briefly records IP addresses to prevent abuse (such as limiting how often orders can be placed) and to count downloads.
  • Browser storage: the site uses no tracking cookies. Your language choice and orders created on this device are kept in your browser’s local storage, readable only by your browser. Clearing site data removes them.

Your rights

Email us anytime to see, correct or delete order information about you. To handle refunds and prevent license abuse, we keep the minimum fields needed for licenses already issued.
