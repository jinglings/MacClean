Install and open MacClean

For macOS 14 and later. Check the package source first. These steps apply only to “developer cannot be verified” or “Apple cannot check for malicious software” alerts.

  1. 1

    Open the DMG and drag to Applications

    Double-click the downloaded MacClean.dmg and drag MacClean to Applications. Eject the DMG after copying finishes.

    MacClean
  2. 2

    Try opening MacClean once

    Double-click MacClean in Applications. If macOS says the developer cannot be verified or Apple cannot check for malicious software, dismiss the alert and continue below.

    macOS cannot verify the developer of “MacClean”.

    Check the source first, then use Privacy & Security.

    Done
  3. 3

    Open Privacy & Security

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, find the notice about MacClean being blocked, and click Open Anyway.

    If the button is missing, first try opening MacClean from Applications, then return here. On a managed Mac, contact your administrator if needed.

    Confirm that you want to open it

    Enter your password or use Touch ID if asked. If you still want to run it, click Open in the next dialog. This adds an exception for MacClean, so subsequent launches will usually work by double-clicking.

    You do not need to turn off system-wide security. Full Disk Access is a separate permission, explained below.

If macOS says “will damage your computer” or “is damaged”

Stop opening the app. Check that it came from the official site, downloaded completely, and has no signing problems. These alerts must not be treated as ordinary unsigned-app warnings. If a fresh download has the same problem, contact support with a screenshot.

Grant Full Disk Access

macOS protects the data folders of apps like Mail and Safari. With access, MacClean can scan them; without it, MacClean still works but some results will be missing.

  1. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access
  2. Turn on the switch next to MacClean (not listed? Click “+” and choose MacClean in Applications)
  3. Quit and reopen MacClean when asked

The Grant button at the bottom of MacClean’s sidebar takes you straight there.
